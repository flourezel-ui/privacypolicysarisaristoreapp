Privacy Policy

Thank you for using Sari-Sari Store Inventory App ("App," "we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect
Information You Provide

The App may collect and store information that you enter, including:

Store name and business information
Product inventory details
Sales records
Customer credit ("Utang") records
Customer names and contact information (if entered)
GCash QR codes uploaded by the store owner
Business notes and transaction history
Automatically Collected Information

Depending on your device and permissions granted, the App may collect:

Device type and operating system
Application version
Error logs and crash reports
Barcode or QR code scan data used within the App
Camera Access

The App may request access to your device camera to:

Scan product barcodes
Scan QR codes
Capture or upload GCash QR codes

Camera data is processed only for the intended functionality and is not accessed without your permission.

Storage Access

The App may request access to device storage to:

Save reports
Export PDF files
Export Excel files
Store backup files
Save uploaded QR code images
2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

Manage inventory records
Process sales transactions
Track customer credits and payments
Generate business reports
Export PDF and Excel documents
Maintain app functionality
Improve user experience
Troubleshoot technical issues
3. Offline Data Storage

The App is designed to operate offline.

Most business data entered into the App is stored locally on your device unless cloud backup features are enabled.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of your business data.

4. Cloud Backup (If Enabled)

If cloud backup or synchronization features are available, business data may be securely stored on third-party cloud services.

Cloud providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. GCash QR Code Storage

If you upload or save a GCash QR code:

The QR code image is stored for payment display purposes.
The App does not process, store, or access your GCash account credentials.
The App does not perform financial transactions on your behalf.
6. Customer Information

Any customer information entered into the App, including credit records and payment history, is stored solely for business management purposes.

Store owners are responsible for obtaining any necessary consent from their customers before storing personal information.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared:

When required by law
To comply with legal obligations
To protect the security and integrity of the App
With cloud service providers if backup services are used
8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information stored within the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained:

As long as it is needed for App functionality
Until deleted by the user
Until the App is uninstalled

Users may delete records at any time through the App.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that information from a child has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

11. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as:

Cloud storage providers
Analytics services
Crash reporting services

These providers may collect limited information necessary to perform their services.

12. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

Access your information
Correct inaccurate information
Delete your information
Export your data
Withdraw permissions previously granted

You may exercise these rights through your device settings or App features.

13. Compliance with Philippine Data Privacy Act

We strive to comply with the requirements of the National Privacy Commission and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) regarding the protection and processing of personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted within the App and reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.
